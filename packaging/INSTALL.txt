Installing Lucky Reference
==========================

The easiest way is the installer: download Lucky-Reference.pkg from the
project's latest release and open it. These steps are for installing by hand
from this zip instead.

1. Copy the plugins into place:
   - "Lucky Reference.vst3"      -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
   - "Lucky Reference.component" -> ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components   (AU, for Logic and GarageBand)

   In Finder, press Cmd+Shift+G and paste the folder path to open it.

2. Unblock the plugins. Lucky Reference is free and not signed by an Apple
   developer account, so macOS blocks it the first time. Open Terminal and run:

   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"Lucky Reference.vst3"
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"Lucky Reference.component"

   Alternatively, if your DAW reports the plugin as blocked, open
   System Settings > Privacy & Security and click "Allow Anyway".

3. Rescan plug-ins in your DAW's settings, or restart it.

4. Put Lucky Reference last on your master track, after any limiter or
   mastering chain, and drop up to three reference tracks onto its slots.
